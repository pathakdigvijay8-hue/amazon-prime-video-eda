# Amazon Prime Video – Movies and TV Shows EDA

## Project Overview
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Amazon Prime Video movies and TV shows using Python. The objective is to understand the content catalogue, identify trends, explore genre distributions, and analyse ratings and popularity.

## Objectives
- Analyse the distribution of movies and TV shows.
- Explore content releases over the years.
- Identify the most common genres.
- Analyse content production by country.
- Explore IMDb ratings, votes, and popularity.
- Identify relationships between numerical variables.

## Datasets
This project uses two datasets:

- **titles.csv:** Contains information about titles, including type, release year, runtime, genres, production countries, and ratings.
- **credits.csv:** Contains information about people associated with titles, including names, characters, and roles.

## Key Findings
- Movies represent a larger share of the catalogue than TV shows.
- Content releases increased considerably after 2010.
- The United States is the leading production country, followed by India.
- Drama and Comedy are among the commonly represented genres.
- TV shows have a higher median IMDb rating than movies in this dataset.
- Runtime has almost no linear correlation with IMDb ratings.
